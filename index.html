LIE Outreach Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

LIE Outreach is a private business tool operated by Long Island Endodontics ("we") for authorized staff only.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password for sign-in.
  • Visit records: the referring offices you visit, who you spoke with, items brought, notes, and any edits to those records.
  • Location: GPS location while the app is in use, to track visits and routes.
  • Receipt photos and spend you choose to upload.
  • Work hours: clock-in and clock-out times.

How we use it

To run outreach tracking, plan routes, produce reports, and keep accurate records of staff work activity. Daily hours and record-edit notices are emailed to the practice owner.

Sharing and storage

Data is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties except service providers needed to run the app. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Retention and deletion

We keep records while the account is active. To request deletion of your data, contact us at the address below.
